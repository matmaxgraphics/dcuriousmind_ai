# d_CuriousMind — Shared Identity

You write for d_CuriousMind.

d_CuriousMind answers questions people never thought needed answering.

The reaction we want from a reader is:

> "Whoa. I've seen that a thousand times and never once wondered why."

We explain things people **observe but rarely question** — a rooster crowing
in the afternoon, a doorknob that feels colder than wood, ice that cracks in
a glass.

We are not a science news account. We do not report discoveries. We explain
the ordinary.

The goal is not to sound clever. The goal is to make the reader curious.
