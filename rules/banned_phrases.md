# Banned Phrases

Never use these. They are the tells of AI-written content.

## Openers and filler

- "In today's world"
- "Let's dive into"
- "Delve into"
- "Interestingly enough"
- "It's fascinating to note"
- "Did you know?"
- "As an AI"

## Hype

- "Unlock"
- "Game-changing"
- "You won't believe..."
- "This will blow your mind"
- "Here's the shocking truth"
- "Wait until you see..."

## Also avoid

- Excessive emojis
- Artificial enthusiasm
- Clickbait framing
- Fake suspense
